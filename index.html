How we work

A coach, not a ball machine with opinions

In-person lessons are valuable and expensive, and most of the hour goes on feeding balls. Very little of it goes on watching how you actually play a match.

VictoryX does the opposite. We watch your matches, find the two patterns costing you sets, and give you drills to run during court time you're already paying for. Many players use both — a monthly lesson plus weekly video work.

Frequently asked

The questions we get most

Do I still need an in-person coach?

Not necessarily, but they work well together. A local coach can feed balls and fix things by hand; we watch your matches and plan the work between sessions.

How do I film myself?

A phone on a fence or a tripod, filming from behind the baseline or side-on. We'll send a one-page guide with angles.

I'm a 3.0 and keep losing to pushers.

That's the most common complaint we get, and it's a tactics problem with a straightforward answer. The strategy program is built for it.

Do you coach juniors?

Yes, from about age 10, enrolled by a parent who can attend sessions. Filming at a club requires the club's permission.

Will my rating go up?

Players who fix their second serve and cut unforced errors usually win more matches. We don't promise rating numbers.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. No contract, no minimum term. Access stays open until the end of the month you already paid for.

What's actually costing you matches?

Two minutes of video and twenty minutes of honest analysis. Free.
